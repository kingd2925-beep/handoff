# Routing policy — edit this to match how YOU work

Label each request **cheap** or **strong**.

**cheap** — a small or free model is good enough:
- summarising or reading long text, first-pass extraction
- drafting variants, rewording, translating, formatting
- simple lookups, quick factual questions, keyword-level file scans
- short conversational replies: acknowledgements, "done", "what next?", status checks
- simple one-step shell or file tasks (list, rename, open, count)

**strong** — needs the best model:
- money math, pricing, anything where a wrong number costs money
- legal, tax, compliance, government rules
- building, debugging or reviewing software; multi-step engineering
- anything sent to a client, published, or public
- final decisions, strategy, critical judgement, design direction
- tasks that change your system, accounts, money or public presence

When mixed, pick the side whose failure would hurt more (usually strong). Judge the request, not its length.
